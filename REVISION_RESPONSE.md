# Scientific revision summary

This record summarises the scientific changes from the supplied
19 October manuscript. Internal AI review is not external human peer review.

## Reader accessibility

Added a proof roadmap, a notation guide and an elementary chronological-tensor
example. The conventional fourth-order proof is retained as a bridge to the
higher-order tree argument. A concluding synthesis identifies the precise
result and remaining question.

## Scope and analytic assumptions

Separated algebraic consistency order from the regularity assumptions needed
for finite-interval global convergence. Clarified the extended-state treatment
of time and the restriction on inexact exponential updates. Retained the
absence of performance and prescribed-stage existence claims.

## Tractable mathematical extension

The earlier generator stopped at the first undetermined weight-seven family.
Exhaustive family enumeration also identifies two aabc families, in addition
to aaabb. The revised manuscript gives a sufficient order-seven criterion
using three explicit tensor equalities. It does not assume these equalities
follow from classical order seven and does not claim their independence on
genuine Williamson lists.

## Evidence and public completeness

Consolidated exact replay, actual child optimisation-mode checks and semantic
corruption controls accompany the existing certificate and independent
implementations. Public provenance, claim mapping, licensing, source roles and
agent navigation are supplied separately. Execution outcomes remain bound to
their exact-version receipts rather than this narrative.

## Preserved limitations

Unrestricted-order transfer, automatic satisfaction of the seventh-order tests,
complete historical priority, proof-assistant formalisation and external human
review are not established by this release.
