# Supplementary bilingual abstracts

These summaries accompany *Order transfer through six for Williamson low-storage Runge–Kutta methods*. The complete hypotheses and proofs are in `paper.md`.

## English

Exponential updates preserve matrix-group constraints, but reusing classical integrator coefficients does not generally preserve accuracy because matrix products do not commute. This study determines when the specified left-multiplication exponential lift of a Williamson recurrence inherits its classical order. A division-free row identity expresses repeated-tail chronological tensors using classical tableau data at every degree. Polarisation, shuffle identities and a continuous integration model then establish automatic transfer through order six for every finite real coefficient list satisfying the corresponding classical conditions, including degenerate lists. A conventional fourth-order calculation provides an elementary entry point, and an exact symbolic certificate checks 197 ordered-tree coefficients through degree six. Exhaustive analysis at weight seven identifies three unresolved tensor directions. Three explicit additional scalar tests suffice for seventh-order transfer, but their automatic satisfaction is not proved. The result removes additional noncommutative order constraints through six for this particular lift; it does not construct coefficient lists or establish performance or stability advantages. Analytic proof, exact computational checks and illustrative examples have distinct evidential roles. No unrestricted-order transfer or counterexample to seventh-order transfer is claimed.

**Keywords:** geometric integration; commutator-free methods; chronological tensors; shuffle identities; rooted trees; exact certificates.

## 繁體中文

指數更新能維持矩陣群的幾何限制，但矩陣乘法不具交換性，因此直接沿用古典積分法係數，未必保有原來的精度。本研究考察 Williamson 遞推式採用指定左乘指數更新時，其階數何時能自動保留。我們以不涉及除法的列恆等式，將任意次數的重複尾項時序張量表示為古典係數表的資料，再結合極化、洗牌恆等式與連續積分模型，證明所有滿足相應古典階數條件的有限實係數列，皆可自動轉移至六階，亦涵蓋退化情形。文中另以傳統記號呈現四階推導，並用精確符號證書檢查至六次的197個有序樹係數。七階的完整分析留下三個未定張量方向；三項明確的附加純量條件足以保證七階轉移，但尚未證明它們自動成立。此結果免除了指定更新法至六階的額外非交換階數限制，並不提供係數構造、效能或穩定性優勢。解析證明、精確計算檢查與示例各有不同的證據作用；本文不宣稱任意階轉移，也未提出七階轉移失敗的反例。

**關鍵詞：** 幾何積分；無交換子方法；時序張量；洗牌恆等式；有根樹；精確證書。
