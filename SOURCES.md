# Primary sources and their roles

1. Williamson (1980), *Low-storage Runge–Kutta schemes*.
   https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-9991(80)90033-9
   Historical low-storage formulation.
2. Owren (2006), *Order conditions for commutator-free Lie group methods*.
   https://doi.org/10.1088/0305-4470/39/19/S15
   Accessible primary preprint:
   https://www.math.ntnu.no/preprint/numerics/2005/N7-2005.pdf
   Ordered tensors and completeness framework used by the proof.
3. Bazavov (2022), *Commutator-free Lie group methods with minimum storage
   requirements and reuse of exponentials*.
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s10543-021-00892-x
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.04225
   Exponential lift and originating conjectural orders.
4. Bazavov (2025), *2N-storage Runge–Kutta methods: c-reflection symmetry and
   factorization of the Butcher tableau*.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08444
   Related reflection operator and the prior rational fourth-order example.
5. Bazavov (2026), *2N-storage Runge–Kutta methods: Order conditions, general
   properties and some analytic solutions*.
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s10543-026-01139-3
   Subsequent coefficient literature and context for the transfer question.
6. Lystad (2026), *All-stage order-three transfer for Williamson 2N-storage
   Runge-Kutta methods*, version 1.0.1.
   https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21764741
   https://github.com/arex1337/williamson-order-three
   Earlier all-stage third-order result, explicitly not claimed as new here.

These sources support specific mathematical or historical relationships, not
blanket endorsements of the candidate. See CITATION_AUDIT.md for provenance
and verification limits.
